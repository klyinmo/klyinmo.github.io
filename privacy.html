Privacy Policy — KLYIN.IQ Games

Effective date: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KLYIN.IQ ("we", "us") handles information in our mobile games, including Meteor Storm, Glow Sort and Block Puzzle (each a "Game"). It applies to every game we publish under the KLYIN.IQ developer name. Some features described below exist only in some of our games. By using a Game you agree to this policy.

1. Information stored on your device

Each Game saves your progress on your device, such as coins, levels, items, missions, settings, language, the player name you type, and a randomly generated player ID. This data stays on your phone unless you use online features (below). Uninstalling the Game, or resetting progress in its settings, deletes it.

2. Online features

Where a Game offers leaderboards, leagues, daily challenges, friends, likes, friend requests or an in-game mailbox, these features use Google Firebase (a service operated by Google). For these features we may store:

  • Your random player ID and friend code
  • Your player name (the name you choose)
  • Game data such as level, experience, scores, rankings, titles and selected items
  • Social data such as likes and friend requests

Your player name, level, scores, rank and similar game data may be visible to other players. We do not ask for your email, phone number, contacts, photos, location or microphone.

3. Advertising

Our Games show ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded ads and ads between rounds or levels. To deliver, measure and (where allowed) personalize ads, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice in the Game. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings.

4. Notifications

If a Game uses push notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging assigns your device a token so we can send game news and gifts. You can turn notifications off in your phone settings at any time.

5. Purchases

Our Games currently have no paid in-app purchases. If purchases are added in the future, payments will be processed only by Google Play; we never receive your card or payment details.

6. How we use information

  • To run the Games, save progress and provide leaderboards and social features
  • To deliver gifts, events and messages
  • To show ads that keep the Games free
  • To keep competitions fair and prevent cheating

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Children

Our Games are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data retention and deletion

Online data is kept while you play. To delete your online data, email us with the Game name and your player ID (shown in the Game's profile or settings) and we will delete it within 30 days. Local data is deleted when you uninstall the Game or reset your progress.

9. Security

We use services from Google (Firebase and AdMob) that protect data with industry-standard security. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

2. الميزات المتصلة بالإنترنت

إذا كانت اللعبة تحتوي على تصنيف أو دوريات أو تحديات يومية أو أصدقاء أو إعجابات أو طلبات صداقة أو بريد داخلي، فهذه الميزات تعتمد على خدمة Google Firebase، وقد نحفظ لها:

  • معرّف اللاعب العشوائي ورمز الصداقة
  • اسم اللاعب الذي تختاره
  • بيانات اللعب مثل المستوى والخبرة والنتائج والترتيب والألقاب والعناصر المختارة
  • البيانات الاجتماعية مثل الإعجابات وطلبات الصداقة

قد يظهر للاعبين الآخرين اسم اللاعب والمستوى والنتائج والترتيب وما يشبهها. لا نطلب بريدك الإلكتروني أو رقم هاتفك أو جهات الاتصال أو الصور أو الموقع أو الميكروفون.

3. الإعلانات

تعرض ألعابنا إعلانات عبر Google AdMob، منها إعلانات اختيارية بمكافأة وإعلانات بين الجولات أو المستويات. ولعرض الإعلانات وقياسها وتخصيصها (حيث يُسمح بذلك) قد تجمع Google معلومات مثل المعرّف الإعلاني للجهاز وعنوان IP ومعلومات الجهاز والتطبيق وتفاعلك مع الإعلانات. للمزيد راجع كيف تستخدم Google المعلومات من المواقع والتطبيقات.

حيث يشترط القانون ذلك (مثل المنطقة الاقتصادية الأوروبية والمملكة المتحدة وسويسرا) نطلب موافقتك قبل عرض الإعلانات المخصّصة، ويمكنك تغيير اختيارك من داخل اللعبة. ويمكنك أيضاً إعادة ضبط المعرّف الإعلاني أو إيقاف الإعلانات المخصّصة من إعدادات أندرويد.

إذا كانت اللعبة تستخدم الإشعارات، تمنح خدمة Firebase Cloud Messaging جهازك رمزاً لنرسل لك أخبار اللعبة والهدايا، ويمكنك إيقافها من إعدادات هاتفك في أي وقت.
